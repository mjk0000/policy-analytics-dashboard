## Original Contributions

This dashboard includes original analysis and methodology:

1. **Financial Resilience Score** - Proprietary scoring system (0-100) measuring ability to absorb financial shocks
2. **Emergency Scenario Testing** - Original framework for testing real-life financial emergencies
3. **Seasonal Stress Calendar** - Unique visualization of monthly financial pressure points
4. **Policy Impact on Resilience** - Novel application of policy research to crisis preparedness

## Methodology Notes

### Our Approach vs Other Calculators

**Economic Policy Institute (EPI) Family Budget Calculator:**
- Purpose: Calculate required income for adequate living standard
- Focus: Income adequacy
- Methodology: Location-specific expense requirements

**MIT Living Wage Calculator:**
- Purpose: Calculate living wage by location
- Focus: Wage adequacy vs minimum wage
- Methodology: Required wage calculations

**Our Affordability Stress Test:**
- Purpose: Measure financial resilience and crisis preparedness
- Focus: Ability to handle financial shocks
- Methodology: Resilience scoring + emergency scenario testing
- **Unique:** Tests financial shock absorption, not just budget adequacy
